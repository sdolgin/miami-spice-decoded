How to read this

Decoded cards have a value score. "Worth" is what the same three courses would cost à la carte at that restaurant, per person, before tax, tip and drinks. ★ Order this marks the highest-value pick in each course. The Full menu tab shows every tracked option with its everyday price.

Confidence & the Limited tag. A green dot means prices came off the restaurant's own current menu. Grey means benchmarked estimates — real enough for ranking, not for arguing with your server. A dashed Limited badge means part of the listing (tier price, days, or menu) hasn't been captured or confirmed yet; a ~ before a price marks the tier price itself as an estimate. Cards marked Limited · menu pending have a confirmed tier price and day schedule (from the GMCVB's own 2026 coverage) but no captured menu, so no value score yet. Restaurants in the "not yet decoded" section come straight from the official participant list with no menu data at all — the link on each goes to their official listing.

The day strip (M–S) shows which days each tier is served. Dashed circles mean days weren't published — filtering by day keeps those in and counts them separately, since we can't rule them in or out. Always call ahead for weekends. "Can't go wrong" means even the cheapest combo of courses still beats the sticker price. Menus change without notice; the source of truth is miamiandbeaches.com.
